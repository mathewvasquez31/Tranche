Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in each tour package?

Each tour package features selected destinations, round-trip transportation, selected local food experience, guided tour of featured stops and travel assistance & support. Package prices and durations vary depending on the destination.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, GCash, and bank transfer for tour payments.

How long does each tour package last?

Tour duration depends on the selected destination. Available packages range from 3 to 5 days, with the specific duration shown on each nation's page.

Can I cancel my tour and request a refund?

Yes. Cancellation and refund requests may be accepted depending on how early the request is made before the scheduled trip. Applicable cancellation fees and refund conditions will be discussed with the traveler before payment is confirmed.
